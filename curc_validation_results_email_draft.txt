Subject: Updated CURC validation results (multi-seed T5-small + Pythia-1.4B attacks)

Hi all,

Attached is a bundle of the CURC validation results we've collected since the
original ICML submission (which only reported seed-42, n=200-subset numbers).
Everything here uses the full test sets. Summary:

- T5-small, 5 seeds (42, 1337, 2024, 8888, 12345): full-test-set ROUGE for
  Standard/Baseline/Monotonic T5 on CNN/DailyMail, XSUM, and SAMSum, with
  bootstrap CIs and paired significance tests. The monotonicity-vs-robustness
  trade-off holds consistently across all 5 seeds, not just seed 42. Most
  seeds also have UAT and order-preservation results; HotFlip attacks are
  complete for one seed and still running for the rest.

- Two attribution-ablation arms (sign_frozen, abs_init_free) at seed 42, to
  isolate whether nonnegativity itself vs. the initialization disruption
  drives the effect. Monotonic training just finished cleanly for both;
  evaluation is next.

- Pythia-1.4B, 3 seeds: UAT attacks, cross-model UAT trigger transfer, and
  HotFlip substitution transfer, all complete. The robustness pattern shows
  up here too (monotonic Pythia sees a smaller NLL increase under attack than
  baseline), consistent with the T5 results.

Not included yet: T5-base/T5-large and Pythia-2.8B/6.9B/Llama-3 scale-up. We
hit a real bug there — the monotonic softplus reparametrization loses
precision catastrophically under bfloat16, which silently corrupted T5-base's
monotonic training. That's fixed now and T5-base is re-running; we're
prioritizing what's needed for a complete submission given the deadline and
treating the larger-scale results as bonus if they land in time.

Full breakdown of what's in the zip is in its README.md.
